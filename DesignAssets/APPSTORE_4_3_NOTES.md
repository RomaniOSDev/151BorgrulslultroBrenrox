# Fieldway — App Store 4.3 notes

## Listing (update in App Store Connect)

**Name:** Fieldway  
**Subtitle:** Seal your cultural field day  
**Keywords:** fieldwork,culture,atlas,seal,travel,journal,drift,brief,relics,map  

**Description (lead):**  
Fieldway is a cultural fieldwork desk—not a generic habit or travel checklist app. Start each day with a Morning Field Brief that carries yesterday’s sealed waymarks forward. Run Atlas map drills and Lattice stories, tag Drift pulls that interrupt you, then close with a Field Day Seal: park up to three findings and a residue note for tomorrow.

**Review path:**  
1. Complete onboarding  
2. Open Atlas → finish any drill (or add a field note)  
3. Brief → Seal the field day → pick ≤3 + residue  
4. Drift → tap a drift tag  
5. Next launch: Morning Field Brief appears on Brief  

No login required.
